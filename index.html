How much does AC installation cost in Florida?

Most installs range from $4,500 to $12,000+, depending on home size, brand, SEER2 rating and complexity. Try the AC Cost Calculator, then compare real quotes so you never overpay.

What SEER2 rating do I need in Florida?

Since January 2023, new split-system central ACs in the Southeast (including Florida) must meet at least 14.3 SEER2 (about 15 SEER) for systems under 45,000 BTU. With Florida's long cooling season, 16 to 20+ SEER2 systems usually pay for themselves in lower bills.

How many quotes should I get?

At least 3. One ACPrices.com form gets you multiple quotes from licensed contractors to compare.

How do I find a licensed HVAC contractor in Florida?

Every contractor in our network is verified Florida-licensed and insured. Fill out the free form and we'll connect you with 2 to 3 qualified contractors within hours.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If your AC is over 10 years old and the repair costs more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is usually smarter. Check typical prices with the AC Repair Cost Calculator or read our repair or replace guide.
